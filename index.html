About

Finding what others miss

I'm a Detection Engineer on Morgan Stanley's Threat Hunt and Cyber Detection team, where I write detections, hunt for threats, and support incident response across the firm.

My path runs through incident response with IBM X-Force, cloud security at Atlas Systems, and security operations at Hevo Data. I hold a Master of Engineering in Cybersecurity from the University of Maryland and GIAC's forensic analyst certifications, GCFA and GNFA.

Outside work I speak at conferences, write about forensics and threat hunting, build open-source detection tooling, and compete in blue-team CTFs.
